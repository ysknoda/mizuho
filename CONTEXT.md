# mizuho

Generates candidate crystal structures from a composition, a space group, and chemical bonding
constraints alone — no energy model, no diffraction data. A candidate is scored by how far it
deviates from the stated bonding geometry, and that score is minimised numerically.

## Language

### Periodicity

**Periodic image**:
A copy of an atom displaced by a whole number of lattice vectors. An atom has infinitely many;
only those within a cutoff matter.
_Avoid_: replica, ghost atom

**Identity image**:
An atom's own zero-shift image, i.e. the atom itself. The only image excluded from every
neighbour count.
_Avoid_: self, self-pair

**Minimum image**:
The single nearest periodic image of one atom as seen from another. A property of an atom
**pair**, and never a coordination number — conflating the two is what made the objective report
NaCl's coordination number as 3 instead of 6.
_Avoid_: nearest neighbour, closest image

**Formula unit count (Z)**:
How many times the composition formula is repeated in the cell. A fixture may deliberately use a
smaller Z than the real mineral to keep the candidate count tractable; lattice constants from a
reduced-Z model have no geometric correspondence to the real cell and must not be compared to it.
_Avoid_: multiplier, repeat count

### Structure generation

**Wyckoff assignment**:
A choice of which Wyckoff position each element occupies. Fixes the structure's topology; the free
coordinates within it are what the optimiser then varies.
_Avoid_: site assignment, configuration

**Orbit**:
The full set of symmetry-equivalent points generated by applying a space group's operations to one
representative point.
_Avoid_: equivalent positions, multiplicity set

**Candidate**:
One Wyckoff assignment together with the parameter vector being optimised for it.
_Avoid_: trial, solution

### Scoring

**Coordination number (CN)**:
How many neighbour **atoms**, counting every periodic image separately, lie within a cutoff of a
centre atom. A cited chemical fact about a mineral, never something re-fitted from our own model.
_Avoid_: neighbour count, ligancy

**Cutoff distance**:
The radius separating a centre atom's first coordination shell from the second. Measured from a
reference structure, not chosen by hand.
_Avoid_: neighbour radius, search radius

**Reference structure**:
A published experimental structure, reconstructed in this repository and verified against its own
bond lengths and coordination numbers before use. The objective is correct only if it is minimised
here.
_Avoid_: ground truth, target structure, experimental data

**Penalty term**:
One named contribution to the total error (overlap, bond, angle, CN, repulsion, charge, volume,
aspect ratio, cell validity, hybridisation).
_Avoid_: energy term, cost component

**Soft coordination number**:
The logistic-weighted neighbour count the coordination term actually evaluates, so the penalty has
a gradient. A numerical device, **not** the definition of a coordination number: a structure can
hold a soft count of 7.187 while its integer coordination is 6.
_Avoid_: CN (unqualified), smoothed CN

**Impostor**:
A structure the objective scores at or below the published one while its lattice is outside the 3%
target. Being zero at the reference structure does not mean being zero *only* there, and every
impostor found so far reproduces its mineral's bond lengths, angles and coordination numbers.
_Avoid_: false minimum, spurious solution, local minimum
